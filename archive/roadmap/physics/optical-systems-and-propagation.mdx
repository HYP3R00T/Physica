## What to study

- [ ] Electromagnetic boundary conditions and Fresnel coefficients; polarization bases, Jones vectors/matrices and birefringence.
- [ ] Fresnel and Fraunhofer diffraction; aperture transforms, resolving power and introductory Fourier optics.
- [ ] Coherence, interference visibility and elementary correlation descriptions.
- [ ] Ray-transfer matrices, paraxial optics, Gaussian beams, resonators and optical waveguides.
- [ ] Dispersion, phase/group velocity, pulse propagation and scattering of light.

## By the end

Choose ray, scalar-wave or electromagnetic methods and calculate a diffraction, polarization or resonator result.
