## What to study

- [ ] State preparation, Born probabilities, observables, measurement and uncertainty relations.
- [ ] Time evolution; Schrödinger equation, stationary states, probability current and continuity.
- [ ] Infinite/finite wells, steps, barriers, tunnelling, bound and scattering states.
- [ ] Harmonic oscillator by differential and ladder-operator methods; wave packets and position/momentum representations.
- [ ] Ehrenfest's theorem and qualified correspondence with classical dynamics.

## By the end

Normalize states, solve standard one-dimensional systems and explain the experimental meaning of calculated probabilities.
