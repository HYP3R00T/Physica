## What to study

- [ ] Interacting gases, virial/cluster expansions and mean-field equations of state.
- [ ] Ising-type models, transfer matrices and one-dimensional exact examples.
- [ ] Order parameters, broken symmetry, Landau theory, correlation functions and critical exponents.
- [ ] Scaling, universality, fluctuations, failure of mean field and introductory renormalization-group calculations.
- [ ] Random walks, Markov processes, master equations, Langevin and Fokker–Planck descriptions.
- [ ] Linear response, fluctuation–dissipation, diffusion and transport.
- [ ] Boltzmann kinetic equation, collision assumptions, relaxation and the limits of equilibrium reasoning.

## By the end

Solve representative interacting and stochastic models and perform a basic scaling or coarse-graining calculation.
