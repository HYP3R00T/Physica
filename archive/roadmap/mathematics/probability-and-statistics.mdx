## What to study

### Probability

- [ ] Random experiments
  - Outcomes and sample spaces
  - Elementary and compound events
  - Impossible and certain events
  - Complementary events
- [ ] Event operations
  - Unions and intersections
  - Mutually exclusive events
  - Exhaustive events
- [ ] Probability rules
  - Equally likely outcomes
  - Addition rule
  - Counting with permutations and combinations
- [ ] Conditional probability
  - Multiplication rule
  - Independent events
  - Total probability
  - Bayes' theorem
- [ ] Discrete random variables
  - Probability mass functions
  - Normalisation
  - Expectation
  - Variance and standard deviation
- [ ] Elementary discrete distributions
  - Bernoulli trials
  - Binomial distribution

### Descriptive statistics

- [ ] Data and frequency distributions
  - Grouped and ungrouped data
  - Frequency tables
  - Histograms
- [ ] Measures of central tendency
  - Mean, median and mode
  - Weighted means
- [ ] Measures of dispersion
  - Mean absolute deviation
  - Variance and standard deviation
  - Distributions with equal means and different variances
