# Segment stages

Assigned for author review on 17 September 2026. All active roadmap segments use the required `stage` field. It replaces `scope` and the former Core, Breadth, Bridge and Integration labels. Archived content is historical and has not been migrated.

## Meaning

- **Foundations:** concepts, laws, models and methods that establish a working understanding of the particular strand.
- **Advanced:** deeper treatments, more demanding models or additional mathematical methods that build on that understanding.

These are editorial judgements about the current checklists. They are not universal academic levels, importance labels, audit status or requirements to finish every segment. A Foundations segment in Quantum field theory can require Advanced segments from Quantum mechanics. Branching, convergence and prerequisites remain independent of stage.

Assign the stage from the dominant treatment of a segment, not its position alone. An introductory mention of a difficult topic does not make the whole segment Advanced. If a checklist combines materially different stages, flag it during the segment-boundary review rather than adding a third label. Do not force equal numbers into each stage.

## Boundary choices

- Classical mechanics: Newtonian motion, conservation, rotation, oscillations, reference frames and central forces remain Foundations. The current Lagrangian treatment and subsequent analytical mechanics are Advanced.
- Electromagnetism: fields, matter response, Maxwell equations and wave propagation remain Foundations. Waveguides, relativistic fields and radiation are Advanced in their current treatments.
- Thermodynamics and statistical mechanics: thermodynamic laws, kinetic theory, ensembles and introductory quantum gases remain Foundations. Critical phenomena and nonequilibrium theory are Advanced.
- Quantum mechanics: the state-space framework, angular momentum, standard approximations, transitions and composite systems remain Foundations. The current scattering, path-integral and open-system treatments are Advanced.
- Relativity: special relativity, relativistic fields, curved spacetime and Einstein equations remain Foundations. Stellar, black-hole, radiation, cosmological and global-evolution treatments are Advanced.
- Waves and acoustics: propagation, boundaries, dispersion and spatial wave behaviour remain Foundations. Radiation, elastic media, acoustic spaces and nonlinear dissipation are Advanced.
- Optics: geometrical optics, imaging systems, interference and diffraction remain Foundations. Fourier imaging and the subsequent crystal, resonator, laser, detection and nonlinear treatments are Advanced. Elementary polarisation itself is not inherently advanced; the present segment includes crystal optics and anisotropic propagation.
- Atomic and molecular physics: atomic structure, spectra, static-field response, bonding and rovibrational spectra remain Foundations. Nonadiabatic dynamics, coherent control, collisions, cooling and precision measurements are Advanced.
- Continuum mechanics and fluid dynamics: balances, material response, ideal and viscous flow remain Foundations. Coupled compressible transport, instabilities and environmental flow models are Advanced.
- Condensed matter physics: crystal structure, phonons, bands, transport and elementary ordered phases remain Foundations. Superconductivity, correlated/topological matter and the current broad soft-matter extension are Advanced. A future independent Soft matter strand would have its own Foundations.
- Electronics and semiconductor devices: junctions, transistors, analogue circuits and digital logic remain Foundations. Precision measurement chains and the combined power, optoelectronic and fabrication extension are Advanced. Simple photodiodes alone do not require that label.
- Nuclear physics: properties, structure, decay, reactions, nuclear energy and detection remain Foundations. Extreme nuclear matter is Advanced.
- Particle physics: particles, processes and experimental observables remain Foundations. The current amplitude-based interaction theories, flavour analysis and beyond-Standard-Model treatment are Advanced. Elementary Standard Model classification already appears in Foundations.
- Quantum field theory: classical fields, quantisation, perturbation theory, functional methods and renormalisation remain Foundations for this strand. Non-Abelian gauge theory with symmetry breaking and nonperturbative directions are Advanced.
- Plasma physics: single-particle, kinetic and fluid descriptions remain Foundations. Detailed wave spectra, instabilities, transport, confinement and space applications are Advanced.
- Astrophysics: observables, radiative processes and stellar structure remain Foundations. The later compact-object, interstellar, galactic and planetary treatments are Advanced in this proposed route.
- Cosmology: background inference, thermal history and linear structure growth remain Foundations. Detailed observable predictions, nonlinear structure, inflation and particle cosmology are Advanced.
- Quantum information: states, circuits and communication remain Foundations. Error correction, fault tolerance and the combined simulation, sensing and hardware treatment are Advanced.
- Mathematics: all five existing segments are Foundations within their respective strands. There is no Advanced quota to fill before later mathematics is written.

## Consolidated segments

The 17 September consolidation preserves all topics and assigns one label to each combined unit. The former per-file stages remain in the [consolidation record](roadmap-consolidation.md). Wave optics and Molecular structure retain Foundations as their dominant entry treatment while including advanced later sections. Stellar physics and compact objects is Advanced because the combined treatment substantially develops compact-object physics. A segment label does not classify every subtopic individually.
