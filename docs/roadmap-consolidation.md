# Roadmap segment consolidation

Proposed mergers applied for author review on 17 September 2026. Physics changes from 156 to 113 segments, retaining all 18 strands. Mathematics retains its five segments. The reviewed proposal was 113; the later conversational reference to 130 was not used as a new target. Consolidation approved for commit on 17 September 2026.

## Preservation and learning order

Eighty source files become 37 coherent units. Each source title remains a section in its combined segment, followed by the original topic and subtopic lists in their original order. Existing subsection headings move down one level. Unmerged bodies are unchanged. Filenames and prerequisite references use the new combined identities.

A prerequisite identifies a segment containing the required knowledge; it does not imply that every subsection is needed. Dependencies internal to a merged unit become its section order. External links target the combined unit. Inherited direct links are removed only when the dependency remains reachable. The resulting graph has been checked for cycles.

Content preservation was checked against a snapshot taken immediately before merging: every substantive body line remains, all checklist and subtopic lines match exactly, and every original prerequisite is either internal to a combined unit or reachable in the new graph. This verifies preservation, not completeness of the physics curriculum.
