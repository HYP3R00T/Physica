## Mathematics

### Inline mathematics

Simple notation should sit comfortably in a sentence: $x$, $0$, $x^2$, $a_n$, $\alpha$, and $\mathbf{v}$.

A fraction $\dfrac{a+b}{c}$ and a root $\sqrt{x^2+y^2}$ check different inline heights.

Longer expressions use display blocks so they do not crowd a paragraph:

$$
\lim_{x\to 0}\dfrac{\sin x}{x}=1
$$

$$
f'(x)=\lim_{h\to 0}\dfrac{f(x+h)-f(x)}{h}
$$

A speed of $2\,\mathrm{m/s}$ checks text inside mathematics.

### Display mathematics

This unnumbered equation checks the default display layout:

$$
\int_0^T v(t)\,\mathrm{d}t = x(T)-x(0)
$$

### Numbered equations and references

The reference to equation \eqref(sample-kinetic-energy) points forward. References should resolve regardless of where they appear in this post.

$$
d = vt
\label{sample-distance}
$$

Equation \eqref(sample-distance) relates distance, speed and time for constant speed.

$$
K = \dfrac{1}{2}mv^2
\label{sample-kinetic-energy}
$$

Compare equations \eqref(sample-distance) and \eqref(sample-kinetic-energy). Repeated references should retain the same numbers.

Labels and references use our custom plugin. Labels go inside display math; references go directly in prose. This code block shows the source literally:

```latex
$$
d = vt
\label{sample-distance}
$$

See equation \eqref(sample-distance).
```

### Aligned equations

$$
\begin{aligned}
v(t) &= v_0 + at \\
x(t) &= x_0 + v_0t + \dfrac{1}{2}at^2
\end{aligned}
\label{sample-constant-acceleration}
$$

Equation \eqref(sample-constant-acceleration) gives two relations in one numbered block.

### Matrices and piecewise functions

$$
\mathbf{A} =
\begin{pmatrix}
1 & 2 & 3 \\
4 & 5 & 6 \\
7 & 8 & 9
\end{pmatrix}
\qquad
\mathbf{x} = \begin{bmatrix}x_1\\x_2\\x_3\end{bmatrix}
$$

$$
|x| =
\begin{cases}
x, & \text{if } x \geq 0, \\
-x, & \text{if } x < 0.
\end{cases}
$$

### Sums, operators and accents

$$
\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\dfrac{x^n}{n!}=e^x
\qquad
\nabla\cdot\mathbf{E}=\dfrac{\rho}{\varepsilon_0}
\qquad
\hat{\mathbf{n}}\cdot\vec{v}=v_n
$$
