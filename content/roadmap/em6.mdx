## What to study

- [ ] Green-function solutions, multipolar sources and time-dependent scalar/vector potentials.
- [ ] Retardation, causal propagation, near and far fields and radiation boundary conditions.
- [ ] Dipole/multipole radiation, angular distributions, radiated power and scattering.
- [ ] Liénard–Wiechert potentials, accelerated charges and relativistic radiation at an introductory working level.
- [ ] Waveguides, cavities, dispersion, response functions and causality/Kramers–Kronig relations.

## By the end

Calculate a radiation field and angular power distribution and solve a guided-mode or causal-response problem.
