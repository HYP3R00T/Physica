## What to study

- [ ] Algebra refresher from Class 10: rearranging equations, ratios, powers, scientific notation, simultaneous linear equations and quadratics. Repair specific gaps; do not restart school mathematics.
- [ ] Functions as input–output rules; domain and range; polynomial, rational, exponential and logarithmic functions; composition and inverse functions.
- [ ] Graphs, slope, intercepts, transformations and inverse graphs; interpreting how one physical variable changes with another.
- [ ] Radians, the unit circle, sine/cosine/tangent graphs, identities, inverse trigonometric functions and small-angle geometry.
- [ ] Coordinates in two and three dimensions; parametric curves; scalars, vectors, components, vector addition, dot products and cross products.

## By the end

Read and sketch elementary functions, resolve vectors and calculate projections without assuming higher-secondary mathematics.
