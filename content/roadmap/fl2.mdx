## What to study

- [ ] Continuum limit, density and flux, local balance laws and material derivatives.
- [ ] Stress, strain, constitutive relations, linear elasticity and elastic waves.
- [ ] Euler and Navier–Stokes equations; incompressibility and viscosity.
- [ ] Reynolds number, dimensional similarity, potential flow, vorticity and low-Reynolds-number examples.
- [ ] Boundary layers, introductory instabilities, compressibility, sound and shock conditions.
- [ ] Turbulent cascades and scaling at introductory depth; why closure and prediction remain difficult.

## By the end

Derive local balance laws and solve representative elastic and fluid models, with introductory treatment of shocks, instabilities and turbulent scaling.
