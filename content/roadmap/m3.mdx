## What to study

- [ ] Complex numbers, conjugates, modulus, argument, polar form, Euler’s formula and complex exponentials.
- [ ] Linear systems, matrices, determinants, vector spaces, bases, eigenvalues, eigenvectors and quadratic forms.
- [ ] First- and second-order ODEs, homogeneous and particular solutions, initial conditions and coupled linear systems.
- [ ] Complex exponentials; underdamped, critically damped and overdamped solutions; driven amplitude, phase and power.

## By the end

Solve driven second-order ODEs and coupled linear systems; interpret eigenvectors and complex amplitudes.
