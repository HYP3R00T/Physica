## What to study

- [ ] Phase-space flow, Poisson brackets, Liouville's theorem and canonical transformations.
- [ ] Generating functions, Hamilton–Jacobi theory, integrability and action–angle variables.
- [ ] Adiabatic invariants, averaging, canonical perturbation ideas, resonances and secular terms.
- [ ] Fixed points, linear stability, phase portraits, bifurcations and limit cycles.
- [ ] Nonlinear oscillators, maps, Poincaré sections, Lyapunov exponents and strange attractors.
- [ ] Hamiltonian versus dissipative chaos; numerical reliability and qualitative limits on prediction.

## By the end

Solve a Hamilton–Jacobi example and analyse stability or chaos quantitatively, including numerical reliability.
