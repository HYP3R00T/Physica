## What to study

- [ ] Degrees of freedom, generalized coordinates, constraints, virtual work and d'Alembert's principle.
- [ ] Lagrange equations, multipliers, generalized potentials and time-dependent constraints; limitations of simple nonholonomic treatments.
- [ ] Symmetries, cyclic coordinates, Noether's theorem and conserved quantities.
- [ ] Central-force reduction, reduced mass, effective potential, Kepler orbits, scattering and the virial theorem.
- [ ] Inertia tensor, principal axes, Euler angles/equations, rigid-body precession and rotating frames.
- [ ] Small oscillations, normal modes, generalized eigenproblems, chains and dispersion.
- [ ] Generalized momenta, Legendre transformations, Hamilton's equations and when the Hamiltonian equals energy.

## By the end

Formulate constrained systems, central-force and rigid-body problems; connect Newtonian, Lagrangian and Hamiltonian descriptions.
