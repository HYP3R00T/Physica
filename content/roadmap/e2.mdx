## What to study

- [ ] Programming basics, arrays, functions, units, plotting, reproducibility and documenting numerical work.
- [ ] Root finding, quadrature, ODE solvers, numerical eigenproblems and Fourier transforms.
- [ ] Round-off, discretization, convergence, stability and comparison with analytic limits.
- [ ] Probability models for data, least-squares fitting, covariance, likelihood, confidence intervals and introductory Bayesian inference.
- [ ] Sensors, calibration, oscilloscope/multimeter use, sampling, aliasing, noise and elementary signal processing.
- [ ] Diodes, transistors, op-amps, filters and basic digital logic as measurement tools; microscopic semiconductor explanations return in module 31.

## By the end

Complete a reproducible numerical study and a dataset analysis. Instrumental competence additionally needs actual equipment practice; data analysis does not replace it.
