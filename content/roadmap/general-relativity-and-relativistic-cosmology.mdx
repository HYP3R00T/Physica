## What to study

- [ ] Equivalence principles, metrics, proper time and geodesics.
- [ ] Stress-energy, Einstein equations, Newtonian limit and the role of conservation identities.
- [ ] Schwarzschild geometry, redshift, light deflection, orbital effects and horizons.
- [ ] FLRW cosmology, Friedmann equations and simple cosmological solutions.
- [ ] Linearized gravity and gravitational waves.

## By the end

Calculate geodesics and interpret Schwarzschild, FLRW and linearized-gravity results at introductory graduate depth.
