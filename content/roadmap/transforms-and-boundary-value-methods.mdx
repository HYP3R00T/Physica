## What to study

- [ ] Fourier series/transforms, convergence and completeness, Laplace transforms, delta functions and convolution.
- [ ] Laplace, Poisson, diffusion and wave equations; initial and boundary conditions; separation of variables.
- [ ] Sturm–Liouville problems, orthogonality, eigenfunction expansions and Green's functions.
- [ ] Legendre polynomials, spherical harmonics, Bessel functions, Gamma/Gaussian integrals and their physical uses.
- [ ] Complex analyticity, Cauchy–Riemann equations, contour integration, Laurent series, residues and branch cuts.

## By the end

Solve representative Laplace, wave and diffusion problems; use eigenfunction expansions, Green functions and contour methods with attention to conditions of validity.
