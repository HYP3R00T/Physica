## What to study

- [ ] Density operators, pure/mixed states, traces and quantum equilibrium ensembles.
- [ ] Bose–Einstein and Fermi–Dirac statistics, occupation numbers and the classical limit.
- [ ] Density of states, ideal Fermi gases, Fermi energy and low-temperature behaviour.
- [ ] Bose gases and condensation; photon and phonon gases, blackbody radiation and heat capacities.
- [ ] Quantum magnetic responses and the limits of independent-particle models.

## By the end

Use density operators and quantum occupations to calculate gas, radiation and heat-capacity observables.
