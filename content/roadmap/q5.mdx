## What to study

- [ ] Schrödinger, Heisenberg and interaction pictures; propagators and time ordering.
- [ ] Density matrices, partial traces, entanglement, Bell inequalities and measurement limitations.
- [ ] Partial-wave scattering, phase shifts, unitarity, optical theorem, resonances and scattering length.
- [ ] Adiabatic evolution, geometric/Berry phase and selected semiclassical methods.
- [ ] Charged particles in fields, gauge transformations, Landau levels and Aharonov–Bohm physics.
- [ ] Path integrals for a particle: time slicing, classical limit and solvable examples.
- [ ] Klein–Gordon and Dirac equations, spinors, nonrelativistic limit and the limits of single-particle relativistic quantum mechanics.

## By the end

Carry out substantial scattering and time-evolution calculations and explain why relativistic particle creation needs field theory.
