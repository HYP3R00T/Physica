## What to study

- [ ] Groups, representations, generators, Lie algebras, SO(3), SU(2) and introductory SU(3) examples.
- [ ] Tensor transformations, invariant quantities and symmetry restrictions on equations and matrix elements.
- [ ] Green functions across mechanics, electromagnetism and quantum theory; spectral and response interpretations.
- [ ] Asymptotic methods, stationary phase, steepest descent and controlled perturbative expansions.

## By the end

Use representations, symmetry, Green functions and asymptotic methods in physical calculations. General abstract algebra and exhaustive analysis are outside this strand.
