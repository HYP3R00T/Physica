## What to study

- [ ] Position, distance, displacement, speed, velocity and acceleration; graphs and initial conditions.
- [ ] Motion in one, two and three dimensions; projectile and circular motion; tangential and normal acceleration.
- [ ] Reference frames, relative motion and Galilean transformations.
- [ ] Newton's laws, inertial frames, force diagrams, gravity near Earth, tension, normal force, springs and friction.
- [ ] Coupled particle constraints, equilibrium, drag and terminal velocity.

## By the end

Draw a force diagram, formulate equations of motion and solve representative one- and two-dimensional problems.
