## What to study

- [ ] Free scalar fields, canonical quantization, creation/annihilation operators and Fock space.
- [ ] Propagators, time ordering, particle processes and introductory S-matrix reasoning.
- [ ] Dirac fields, fermionic statistics and the physical interpretation of antiparticles.
- [ ] Interactions, perturbation expansions and simple Feynman-diagram calculations.
- [ ] Gauge fields and introductory QED; regularization, renormalization and scale dependence in a worked simple example.
- [ ] Symmetry breaking and the relationship between field models and particle/condensed-matter phenomena.

## By the end

Quantize a free field and calculate a simple lowest-order process and a basic renormalization example.
