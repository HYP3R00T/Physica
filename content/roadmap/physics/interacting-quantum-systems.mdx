## What to study

- [ ] Second quantization for bosons and fermions; many-body Hamiltonians.
- [ ] Mean-field and Hartree–Fock approximations; density-functional theory as a framework and its limitations.
- [ ] Correlations, Green functions, spectral functions and linear response.
- [ ] Hubbard/Heisenberg models, elementary excitations and interacting electrons.
- [ ] Cooper pairing, Bogoliubov methods and a worked basic superconducting or Bose-gas model.

## By the end

Construct a second-quantized model and solve a worked mean-field, pairing or Bose-gas approximation.
