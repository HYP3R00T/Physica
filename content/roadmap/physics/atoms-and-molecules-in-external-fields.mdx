## What to study

### Magnetic-field response

- [ ] Atomic magnetic structure
    - Magnetic moments and Landé factors
    - Weak-field Zeeman splitting
    - Hyperfine Zeeman structure and Breit–Rabi behaviour
    - Paschen–Back regimes and level mixing
    - Diamagnetic contributions
- [ ] Magnetic resonance
    - Spin precession and resonance conditions
    - Electron-spin and nuclear-spin resonance
    - Magnetic selection rules and state preparation

### Electric-field response

- [ ] Stark structure
    - Permanent and induced electric moments
    - Linear and quadratic Stark effects
    - Scalar, vector and tensor polarisabilities
    - Degeneracy, avoided crossings and state mixing
- [ ] Molecular field response
    - Molecular dipole moments and rotational states
    - Orientation and alignment
    - Stark shifts and rotational-level mixing
    - Molecular magnetic moments

### Time-dependent and strong fields

- [ ] Oscillating-field shifts
    - Dynamic polarisability and AC Stark shifts
    - Near-resonant and far-detuned regimes
    - Ponderomotive energy
- [ ] Excited states in fields
    - Rydberg-state sensitivity and field ionisation
    - Adiabatic following and avoided-crossing transitions
    - Perturbative and nonperturbative regimes
