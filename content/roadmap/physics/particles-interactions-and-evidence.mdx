## What to study

- [ ] Relativistic kinematics, scattering, decays, invariant mass and phase space at an introductory level.
- [ ] Leptons, quarks, hadrons and gauge bosons; charges and conserved quantum numbers.
- [ ] Isospin, flavour multiplets, discrete symmetries, parity violation and CP violation.
- [ ] QED, weak interactions, QCD and the electroweak/Higgs framework as a physical overview.
- [ ] Neutrino mixing/oscillations, experimental signatures, accelerators and detector principles.

## By the end

Interpret elementary scattering and decay processes and connect conservation rules with observations; full Standard Model amplitudes come later.
