## What to study

### Optical gain

- [ ] Light and matter transitions
    - Absorption, spontaneous emission and stimulated emission
    - Transition energies and Einstein coefficients
    - Population inversion and pumping
    - Gain cross sections and stimulated-emission rates
    - Homogeneous and inhomogeneous broadening
    - Gain bandwidth and saturation
- [ ] Gain media and level schemes
    - Two-level limitations
    - Three-level and four-level models
    - Radiative and nonradiative relaxation
    - Optical, electrical and collisional pumping
    - Gas, solid-state, fibre and semiconductor laser principles

### Laser operation

- [ ] Threshold and steady state
    - Round-trip gain and loss
    - Population and photon rate equations
    - Threshold pump level
    - Output coupling and slope efficiency
    - Gain clamping and spatial hole burning
    - Amplifiers and amplified spontaneous emission
- [ ] Modes and dynamics
    - Longitudinal and transverse mode selection
    - Mode competition and gain narrowing
    - Relaxation oscillations
    - Frequency and intensity noise: introduction
    - Laser linewidth and coherence
    - Thermal lensing and stability limits

### Pulsed operation

- [ ] Pulse generation
    - Gain switching
    - Q-switching and stored inversion
    - Active and passive mode locking
    - Pulse duration, bandwidth and time-bandwidth product
    - Dispersion compensation: introduction
    - Peak power, pulse energy and average power
- [ ] Model boundaries
    - Rate-equation and coherent-interaction regimes
    - Population relaxation and optical dephasing
    - Optical Bloch equations: introduction
    - Semiclassical fields and quantum-noise limitations
