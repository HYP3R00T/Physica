## What to study

### Interference of light

- [ ] Superposition and optical intensity

- [ ] Two-beam interference
    - Division of wavefront and division of amplitude
    - Young's double slit
    - Fresnel biprism and Lloyd's mirror
    - Thin films, wedges and Newton's rings
    - Phase changes on reflection
    - Localised and nonlocalised fringes
