## What to study

### Interaction potentials

- [ ] Long-range interactions
    - Permanent multipoles and induction
    - Dispersion forces and van der Waals coefficients
    - Retardation and the Casimir–Polder regime
    - Anisotropic molecular interactions
- [ ] Short-range interactions
    - Electronic exchange and repulsive cores
    - Potential curves and collision channels
    - Elastic, inelastic and reactive processes

### Scattering descriptions

- [ ] Collision observables
    - Differential and integral cross sections
    - Scattering amplitudes and partial waves
    - Rate coefficients and velocity averaging
    - Identical-particle symmetry and spin channels
- [ ] Multichannel dynamics
    - Coupled-channel equations
    - Rotational, vibrational and electronic excitation
    - Charge exchange and electron-impact processes
    - Threshold laws and scattering lengths
    - Shape and Feshbach resonances
- [ ] Collision outcomes
    - Quenching and collisional broadening
    - Ionisation and recombination
    - Energy transfer and reaction branching
    - Two-body and three-body loss processes
    - Low-energy and semiclassical limits
