## What to study

### Angular description of circular motion

- [ ] Circular paths
    - Fixed centre and constant radius
    - Particle motion and rigid-body rotation distinctions
- [ ] Angular position and displacement
    - Radian measure and arc length
    - Angular orientation and sign conventions
- [ ] Angular velocity and acceleration about a fixed axis
    - Average and instantaneous angular velocity
    - Angular speed and direction of traversal
    - Average and instantaneous angular acceleration
    - Angular displacement from angular velocity
    - Constant-angular-acceleration relations
- [ ] Linear and angular quantities
    - Tangential speed and angular speed
    - Tangential acceleration and angular acceleration
    - Period and frequency of uniform circular motion

### Velocity and acceleration

- [ ] Radial and tangential directions
    - Changing directions along the path
    - Velocity tangent to the circle
- [ ] Radial acceleration
    - Inward direction
    - Speed, radius and angular-speed dependence
    - Changing velocity at constant speed
- [ ] Tangential acceleration
    - Changes in speed
    - Speeding up and slowing down
- [ ] Total acceleration
    - Perpendicular radial and tangential components
    - Magnitude and direction
    - Uniform and nonuniform circular motion

### Force equations in an inertial frame

- [ ] Radial force balance
    - Net inward force and radial acceleration
    - Centripetal force as the radial resultant
    - Contributions from tension, normal force, friction and gravity
- [ ] Tangential force balance
    - Net tangential force and speed change
    - Work and kinetic-energy change
    - Zero work by a purely radial force on a fixed circular path
- [ ] Constraint conditions
    - Taut strings and nonnegative tension
    - Contact and nonnegative normal reaction
    - Available static friction and onset of slipping
    - Loss of the circular-path constraint

### Horizontal circular-motion models

- [ ] Particle attached to a string on a smooth horizontal surface
    - Tension and prescribed circular speed
    - Tangential velocity at release
- [ ] Conical pendulum
    - Vertical force balance and horizontal radial force
    - String angle, circle radius and speed
- [ ] Vehicle turning on a level road
    - Static friction as the radial force
    - Limiting speed without slipping
- [ ] Banked turns
    - Normal-force components
    - Design speed without friction
    - Friction direction above and below the design speed
    - Allowed speed range with static friction

### Vertical circular-motion models

- [ ] Particle on a string in a vertical circle
    - Radial and tangential components of gravity
    - Speed changes from mechanical-energy conservation
    - Tension at different positions
    - Limiting conditions for a complete circle
    - Slackening and subsequent projectile motion
- [ ] Particle on a smooth vertical circular track
    - Inside and outside contact
    - Normal reaction and energy conservation
    - Contact-loss conditions
- [ ] Particle constrained by an ideal rigid rod
    - Tension and compression
    - Rod and string constraint distinctions

### Circular gravitational orbits

- [ ] Test mass orbiting a fixed spherical source
    - Exterior inverse-square gravitational field
    - Gravity as the radial force
    - Circular orbital speed and period
    - Orbital radius measured from the source centre
    - Kinetic, potential and total orbital energy
    - Free fall in orbit and apparent weightlessness
