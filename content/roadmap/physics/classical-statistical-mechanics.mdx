## What to study

- [ ] Microstates, macrostates, phase space, density of states and statistical entropy.
- [ ] Equilibrium assumptions, ergodicity as a qualified hypothesis, thermodynamic limit and ensemble reasoning.
- [ ] Microcanonical, canonical and grand canonical ensembles; partition functions and chemical potential.
- [ ] Ideal gases, equipartition, Gibbs paradox, two-level systems and paramagnets.
- [ ] Fluctuations, susceptibilities and the relation between statistical and thermodynamic descriptions.

## By the end

Construct ensembles and derive macroscopic quantities and fluctuations from a specified microscopic model.
