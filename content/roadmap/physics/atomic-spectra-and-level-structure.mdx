## What to study

### Energy-level splittings

- [ ] Fine structure
    - Relativistic kinetic and spin-orbit corrections
    - Darwin term and hydrogenic fine structure
    - Multiplet splittings and coupling regimes
- [ ] Nuclear contributions
    - Nuclear spin and magnetic moments
    - Hyperfine coupling and total angular momentum
    - Magnetic-dipole and electric-quadrupole hyperfine structure
    - Isotope shifts, mass shifts and field shifts
- [ ] Radiative corrections: introduction
    - Lamb shift and anomalous magnetic moment
    - Experimental signatures and quantum-electrodynamic origin

### Atomic transitions

- [ ] Transition classification
    - Electric-dipole selection rules
    - Magnetic-dipole and electric-quadrupole transitions
    - Allowed, forbidden and metastable transitions
    - Angular factors, reduced matrix elements and line strengths
- [ ] Spectral intensities
    - Oscillator strengths and sum rules
    - Einstein coefficients and branching ratios
    - Level populations and fluorescence yields
    - Radiative lifetimes and natural linewidths
- [ ] Spectral interpretation
    - Term diagrams and combination relations
    - Rydberg-series assignments
    - Resonance lines and fine-structure multiplets
    - Inner-shell transitions, characteristic X-rays and Auger decay
    - Autoionising states and asymmetric resonances

### Line profiles

- [ ] Broadening and shifts
    - Natural, Doppler and collisional broadening
    - Lorentzian, Gaussian and Voigt profiles
    - Pressure shifts and instrumental broadening
    - Homogeneous and inhomogeneous contributions
