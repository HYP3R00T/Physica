## What to study

### Small oscillations of coupled systems

- [ ] Equilibrium and linearisation
    - Stationary potential energy
    - Quadratic kinetic and potential energies
    - Mass and stiffness matrices
    - Potential-energy Hessian at equilibrium
    - Small motion about a static equilibrium
    - Positive-definite mass matrices and independent coordinates
- [ ] Normal-mode problem
    - Generalised eigenvalue equation
    - Secular equation and characteristic determinant
    - Natural frequencies and mode shapes
    - Mass-weighted orthogonality and normalisation
    - Degenerate frequencies
    - Zero-frequency modes and continuous symmetries
    - Accidental zero modes and higher-order stability analysis
    - Negative squared frequencies and instability
- [ ] Normal coordinates
    - Modal transformation
    - Decoupled harmonic equations
    - Initial conditions and mode amplitudes
    - Modal energy and superposition

### Coupled oscillator models

- [ ] Two coupled oscillators
    - Symmetric and antisymmetric modes
    - Unequal masses and stiffnesses
    - Weak coupling and energy exchange
    - Beats and near-resonant transfer
- [ ] Multiple mechanical oscillators
    - Coupled pendulums
    - Small oscillations of a double pendulum
    - Finite mass–spring chains
    - Fixed, free and periodic boundary conditions

### Driving and damping in coupled systems

- [ ] Forced response
    - Modal forcing and excitation strength
    - Resonances and antiresonances
    - Transient and steady-state motion
- [ ] Damped response
    - Damping matrices
    - Proportional damping and independent modal equations
    - Non-proportional damping and coupled modal response
    - Limits of undamped-mode approximations

### From discrete systems to waves

- [ ] Large oscillator chains
    - Wave number and dispersion relation
    - Phase and group velocity
    - Long-wavelength approximation
- [ ] Continuum limit
    - Field displacement and distributed mass
    - Wave equation from a mass–spring chain
    - Standing modes and boundary conditions
    - Discrete-mode and continuum descriptions
- [ ] Energy and action of a continuous mechanical system
    - Kinetic and potential-energy densities
    - Lagrangian density for an ideal vibrating string
    - Field Euler–Lagrange equation
    - Initial conditions, boundary conditions and normal modes
    - Local energy balance and energy flux
