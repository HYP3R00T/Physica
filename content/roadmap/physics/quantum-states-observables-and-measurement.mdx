## What to study

### State spaces and operators

- [ ] Hilbert-space descriptions
    - State vectors, rays and inner products
    - Dirac notation and dual vectors
    - Orthonormal bases, completeness and basis changes
    - Discrete and continuous representations
    - Kronecker and Dirac delta normalisation
    - Generalised eigenstates and resolutions of the identity
- [ ] Observables and spectra
    - Linear operators, adjoints and self-adjoint observables
    - Eigenvalues, eigenvectors and degeneracy
    - Spectral decompositions and projection operators
    - Operator domains and boundary conditions
- [ ] Compatible and incompatible observables
    - Commutators and simultaneous eigenstates
    - Complete sets of commuting observables
    - Canonical position-momentum commutation relations
    - Robertson and Schrödinger uncertainty relations

### Measurement and preparation

- [ ] Measurement postulates
    - Born probabilities and projective measurements
    - State update and sequential measurements
    - Degenerate outcomes and subspace projections
    - Preparation uncertainty and measurement uncertainty
- [ ] Pure states and statistical mixtures
    - Density operators and ensemble descriptions
    - Trace, positivity and normalisation
    - Expectation values and purity
    - Coherent superpositions and incoherent mixtures

### Operator dynamics

- [ ] Unitary evolution
    - Evolution operators and composition
    - Time-independent and time-dependent Hamiltonians
    - Time ordering
    - Schrödinger and Heisenberg pictures
    - Constants of motion and Heisenberg equations
    - Von Neumann equation for density-operator evolution
- [ ] Energy-time uncertainty
    - Evolution timescales and energy spread
    - Mandelstam–Tamm relation
    - Time as an evolution parameter in ordinary Schrödinger mechanics
    - Lifetime broadening and its distinction from position-momentum uncertainty
- [ ] Oscillator operator methods
    - Creation and annihilation operators
    - Number states and ladder construction
    - Coherent states and displacement operators
    - Minimum uncertainty and oscillator dynamics
- [ ] Two-state systems
    - Matrix Hamiltonians and level mixing
    - Avoided crossings
    - Bloch-sphere representations
    - Population and phase evolution
