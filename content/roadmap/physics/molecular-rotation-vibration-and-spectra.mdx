## What to study

### Rotational structure

- [ ] Molecular rotors
    - Diatomic rigid rotor
    - Rotational constants and moments of inertia
    - Centrifugal distortion
    - Linear, symmetric-top and asymmetric-top molecules
    - Rotational degeneracies and selection rules
- [ ] Nuclear statistics
    - Nuclear-spin symmetry and rotational populations
    - Ortho and para species
    - Statistical weights in molecular spectra

### Vibrational structure

- [ ] Diatomic vibrations
    - Harmonic approximation and zero-point motion
    - Anharmonicity and Morse potential
    - Overtones, hot bands and dissociation
    - Vibration-rotation coupling
- [ ] Polyatomic vibrations
    - Normal coordinates and normal modes
    - Vibrational symmetry and degeneracy
    - Infrared and Raman activity
    - Anharmonic coupling and Fermi resonance

### Molecular spectroscopy

- [ ] Rotation-vibration spectra
    - Branch structure and line assignments
    - Transition moments and intensity patterns
    - Isotope substitution and structural information
    - Effective Hamiltonians and fitted constants
- [ ] Raman spectra
    - Polarisability changes and Raman selection rules
    - Rotational and vibrational Raman scattering
    - Stokes and anti-Stokes lines
- [ ] Coupled angular motion
    - Electronic, rotational and spin angular momenta
    - Hund's coupling cases
    - Spin-rotation coupling and lambda doubling
    - Coriolis coupling and perturbations
