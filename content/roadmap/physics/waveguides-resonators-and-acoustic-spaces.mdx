## What to study

### Guided propagation

- [ ] Transverse confinement
    - Separation of longitudinal and transverse coordinates
    - Transverse eigenfunctions and boundary conditions
    - Modal orthogonality and expansion
    - Cutoff frequencies and longitudinal dispersion
    - Propagating and evanescent modes
- [ ] Acoustic ducts
    - Rigid-wall rectangular and circular guides
    - Plane-wave and higher-mode regimes
    - Phase velocity, group velocity and modal power
    - Source coupling and mode selection
    - Changes in cross section and mode conversion
    - Slowly varying horns and Webster's equation
- [ ] Elastic waveguides
    - Rod and plate modes
    - Symmetric and antisymmetric Lamb waves
    - Shear-horizontal plate modes
    - Thickness-dependent dispersion
    - Leakage into a surrounding medium

### Resonators and coupled cavities

- [ ] Standing modes in bounded domains
    - Rectangular and cylindrical cavities
    - Circular membranes and Bessel-function modes
    - Degeneracy and symmetry
    - Source position and excitation strength
- [ ] Lumped acoustic elements
    - Acoustic inertance, compliance and resistance
    - Pressure and volume-velocity variables
    - Helmholtz resonator
    - Neck end corrections and cavity-size assumptions
    - Coupled resonators and frequency splitting
- [ ] Resonance and loss
    - Resonance width and quality factor
    - Radiation, wall and internal losses
    - Loaded and intrinsic quality factors
    - Ring-down and driven response
    - Resonant absorption and impedance matching

### Enclosed sound fields

- [ ] Direct sound and reflections
    - Early reflections and echoes
    - Room modes and spatial variation
    - Diffuse-field approximation
    - Modal and statistical descriptions
- [ ] Reverberation
    - Energy decay and reverberation time
    - Absorption coefficient and equivalent absorption area
    - Sabine and Eyring estimates
    - Assumptions and limits of reverberation formulas
    - Frequency dependence and nonuniform absorption
- [ ] Transmission and isolation
    - Transmission loss and insertion loss distinctions
    - Single partitions and mass-law approximation
    - Structural resonances and coincidence effects
    - Absorption and sound isolation distinctions

### Acoustic measurements

- [ ] Response measurements
    - Impulse response and transfer function
    - Frequency response and spectral resolution
    - Windowing, background noise and calibration
    - Decay measurements and mode identification
