## What to study

### Perturbation theory

- [ ] Nondegenerate perturbations
    - Expansion of energies and eigenstates
    - First-order and second-order energy corrections
    - Normalisation and convergence limits
    - Anharmonic oscillator corrections
- [ ] Degenerate and nearly degenerate perturbations
    - Perturbation matrices within degenerate subspaces
    - Level splitting, mixing and avoided crossings
    - Effective Hamiltonians
- [ ] Atomic perturbations
    - Linear and quadratic Stark effects
    - Zeeman effect and strong-field crossover
    - Relativistic kinetic, spin-orbit and Darwin corrections
    - Hydrogen fine structure
    - Nuclear spin and hyperfine structure: introduction
    - Limits of nonrelativistic corrections

### Variational methods

- [ ] Variational principle
    - Ground-state energy bounds
    - Trial states and parameter optimisation
    - Rayleigh–Ritz basis expansions
    - Excited states and orthogonality constraints
    - Basis convergence and approximation errors
- [ ] Parameter dependence
    - Hellmann–Feynman theorem
    - Energy derivatives and expectation values
    - Eigenstate and degeneracy conditions

### Semiclassical bound states and tunnelling

- [ ] WKB approximation
    - Local wavelength and slowly varying potentials
    - Allowed and forbidden regions
    - Turning points and connection formulae
    - Bound-state quantisation conditions
    - Barrier transmission and tunnelling exponents
    - Breakdown near turning points and barriers
- [ ] Metastable states
    - Quasibound levels and escape rates
    - Energy widths and lifetimes
    - Semiclassical decay models
