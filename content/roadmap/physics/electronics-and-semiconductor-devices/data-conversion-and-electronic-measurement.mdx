## What to study

### Mixed-signal systems

- [ ] Sampling
    - Sampling theorem
    - Aliasing
    - Sample-and-hold circuits
    - Antialias filters

- [ ] Conversion
    - Digital-to-analogue converters
    - Analogue-to-digital converters
    - Quantisation noise
    - Resolution, linearity and conversion speed

### Transducers and measurement chains

- [ ] Transducer characteristics
    - Sensitivity, range, linearity and hysteresis
    - Response time, loading and calibration
    - Selecting a sensor for the measured quantity and environment

- [ ] Temperature and pressure measurements
    - Thermocouples, resistance thermometers and thermistors
    - Capacitive and strain-gauge pressure transducers
    - Thermal-conductivity and ionisation vacuum gauges
    - Working ranges and calibration limits

- [ ] Magnetic, mechanical and radiation measurements
    - Hall probes, search coils and fluxgate magnetometers
    - Piezoelectric vibration sensors and accelerometers
    - Photodiodes, photomultipliers and optical responsivity
    - Particle-detector charge signals, efficiency and counting statistics

- [ ] Sensor interfaces
    - Bridge circuits
    - Transimpedance amplifiers
    - Charge-sensitive amplifiers
    - Impedance matching

- [ ] Detection
    - Lock-in amplification
    - Phase-sensitive detection
    - Boxcar integration, gated sampling and synchronous averaging
    - Amplitude, frequency and phase modulation
    - Demodulation, reference signals and recovery bandwidth
    - Pulse shaping
    - Triggering and counting

- [ ] Measurement quality
    - Calibration
    - Bandwidth and settling
    - Grounding and shielding
    - Uncertainty and noise budgets

### High-frequency measurement and control

- [ ] High-frequency sources and detectors
    - Radio-frequency and microwave signal generators
    - Diode detectors, mixers and heterodyne detection
    - Oscilloscope and spectrum-analyser bandwidth
    - Transmission-line matching, reflections and termination
- [ ] Measurement and control loops
    - Setpoints, error signals and feedback sensors
    - Proportional, integral and derivative control
    - Stability, response time, overshoot and actuator limits
    - Acquisition timing and digital control latency

### Experimental data analysis

- [ ] Calibration and uncertainty
    - Calibration curves and reference uncertainties
    - Repeated measurements and correlated errors
    - Propagating uncertainty through the measurement chain
    - Systematic offsets and drift
- [ ] Fitting experimental models
    - Linear and nonlinear least-squares fits
    - Weighting observations by measurement uncertainty
    - Parameter uncertainty and covariance
    - Residuals, chi-square goodness-of-fit and degrees of freedom
    - Outliers, model inadequacy and limits of extrapolation
