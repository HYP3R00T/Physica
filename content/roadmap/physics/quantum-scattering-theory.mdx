## What to study

### Scattering states and observables

- [ ] Asymptotic scattering descriptions
    - Incoming waves, outgoing waves and scattering amplitudes
    - Probability flux and differential cross sections
    - Total and transport cross sections
    - Laboratory and centre-of-mass coordinates
    - Angular and cross-section transformations between frames
    - Elastic and inelastic channels
- [ ] Scattering operators
    - Free and interacting asymptotic states
    - Scattering matrix and transition matrix
    - Unitarity and conservation of probability
    - Optical theorem

### Potential scattering

- [ ] Integral-equation methods
    - Outgoing Green functions
    - Lippmann–Schwinger equation
    - Born series and first Born approximation
    - Fourier transforms of scattering potentials
    - Weak-potential and high-energy validity limits
- [ ] Partial-wave methods
    - Angular decomposition and phase shifts
    - Partial-wave cross sections and unitarity bounds
    - Low-energy scattering and scattering length
    - Effective range and threshold behaviour
    - Bound states, resonances and phase shifts
    - Levinson theorem and threshold qualifications
- [ ] Representative scattering models
    - Hard spheres and finite-range wells
    - Resonant scattering and resonance widths
    - Coulomb scattering and long-range asymptotic modifications
    - Identical-particle interference and spin dependence
- [ ] Multichannel scattering
    - Channel thresholds and coupled-channel equations
    - Inelastic transitions and channel probabilities
    - Resonance lifetimes and time delays
