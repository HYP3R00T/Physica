## What to study

- [ ] Charge, Coulomb force, electric field, electric flux, Gauss's law, potential and electrostatic energy.
- [ ] Conductors, capacitance, dielectric response and stored energy.
- [ ] Build continuous-charge fields and potentials from small charge elements using ordinary integrals; introduce flux geometrically and use Gauss’s law for symmetric cases. General surface calculus comes later.

## By the end

Calculate elementary fields, potentials and capacitances; distinguish field, force, potential and energy.
