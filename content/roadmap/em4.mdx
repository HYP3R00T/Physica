## What to study

- [ ] Electrostatic boundary conditions and uniqueness; images, separation of variables and multipole expansions.
- [ ] Polarization, bound charge, displacement field, dielectric interfaces and electrostatic energy.

- [ ] Maxwell equations in differential and integral form; induction and charge continuity.

## By the end

Solve nontrivial static boundary problems and use Maxwell’s equations in integral and differential forms.
