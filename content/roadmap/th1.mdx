## What to study

- [ ] Temperature, thermal equilibrium, thermal expansion, heat capacity and calorimetry.
- [ ] Ideal-gas model, elementary kinetic theory and molecular explanations of pressure.
- [ ] Heat, work, internal energy and the first law; common thermodynamic processes.
- [ ] Heat engines, refrigerators, the second law, reversible processes and entropy changes.
- [ ] Conduction, convection and thermal radiation at introductory depth.

## By the end

Calculate heat balances and simple cycles, and distinguish heat, temperature, work, energy and entropy.
