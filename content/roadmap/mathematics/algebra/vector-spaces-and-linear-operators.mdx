## What to study

### Vector spaces

- [ ] Real and complex vector spaces
    - Subspaces, span and linear independence
    - Bases, dimension and coordinate changes
    - Polynomial and function-space examples
- [ ] Linear maps
    - Kernel, image and rank-nullity theorem
    - Matrix representations and change of basis
    - Dual spaces and linear functionals

### Inner products and projections

- [ ] Inner-product spaces
    - Norms, orthogonality and Cauchy-Schwarz inequality
    - Gram-Schmidt orthogonalisation
    - Orthogonal complements and projections
    - Orthonormal bases and completeness in finite dimensions
- [ ] Least-squares approximation
    - Projection onto a column space
    - Normal equations and QR factorisation
    - Overdetermined systems and residuals

### Operator structure

- [ ] Adjoint operators
    - Self-adjoint, unitary and normal operators
    - Finite-dimensional spectral theorem
    - Commuting operators and simultaneous diagonalisation
- [ ] General spectral decompositions
    - Generalised eigenvectors and Jordan form
    - Singular value decomposition and pseudoinverses
    - Matrix functions and matrix exponentials
    - Positive operators and variational eigenvalue bounds
