## What to study

### Adjoints and the spectral theorem

- [ ] Operators on inner-product spaces
    - Adjoint maps, transpose and conjugate transpose
    - Self-adjoint, orthogonal, unitary and normal operators
    - Finite-dimensional spectral theorem
    - Orthogonal and unitary diagonalisation
    - Spectral projectors and simultaneous diagonalisation of commuting self-adjoint operators
- [ ] Quadratic and Hermitian forms
    - Real symmetric and complex Hermitian matrices
    - Principal axes, signature and definiteness
    - Positive definite and positive semidefinite operators
    - Eigenvalue and leading-principal-minor tests with their assumptions
    - Cholesky factorisation
    - Rayleigh quotients and variational eigenvalue bounds

### Singular values and approximation

- [ ] Singular value decomposition
    - Singular values and left and right singular vectors
    - Full and reduced forms of the decomposition
    - Rank and the fundamental subspaces
    - Moore-Penrose pseudoinverse and minimum-norm least-squares solutions
    - Eckart-Young theorem, low-rank approximation and principal components
    - Polar decomposition and its geometric interpretation
- [ ] Conditioning and computation
    - Vector and induced matrix norms
    - Singular values and condition numbers
    - Sensitivity of linear systems and residual versus solution error
    - Forward error, backward error and floating-point roundoff
    - Stable factorisations and choosing a solver for the matrix structure

### General operators

- [ ] Beyond diagonalisation
    - Schur triangularisation over the complex numbers and real Schur form
    - Generalised eigenspaces, eigenvectors and Jordan chains
    - Jordan canonical form over the complex numbers
    - Matrix functions on Jordan blocks
    - Distinguishing structural canonical forms from numerically stable decompositions

### Numerical solution methods

- [ ] Iteration theory for linear systems
    - Matrix splittings, fixed-point iteration and error propagation
    - Spectral-radius convergence criterion for stationary iterations
    - Residual-based stopping, tolerances and convergence versus stagnation
- [ ] Stationary iterative solvers
    - Jacobi, Gauss-Seidel and successive over-relaxation (SOR)
    - Diagonal dominance as a sufficient convergence condition for Jacobi and Gauss-Seidel
    - Symmetric positive definite systems, Gauss-Seidel convergence and SOR parameter restrictions
- [ ] Krylov subspace methods
    - Conjugate gradients for symmetric positive definite systems
    - GMRES for general nonsymmetric systems
    - Preconditioning, sparse matrices and comparing computational costs
- [ ] Computing eigenvalues and singular values
    - Power iteration, inverse iteration and shifts
    - QR iteration and the role of orthogonal transformations
    - Stable numerical computation versus symbolic canonical forms

Algorithm implementation and further error analysis continue in [numerical approximation and linear algebra](#segment-numerical-approximation-and-linear-algebra).

Lang, Chapter VIII, for symmetric operators; Strang, Chapters 5-7 and Appendix B, for positive definiteness, singular values, conditioning and Jordan form.
