## What to study

### Sets and logical statements

- [ ] Sets and membership
    - Subsets, power sets and Cartesian products
    - Union, intersection, difference and complement
    - Finite, countable and uncountable sets
- [ ] Mathematical statements
    - Logical connectives and truth tables
    - Quantifiers and negation
    - Necessary and sufficient conditions

### Relations and functions

- [ ] Relations
    - Reflexivity, symmetry and transitivity
    - Equivalence relations, classes and partitions
    - Order relations
- [ ] Functions
    - Domain, codomain, image and preimage
    - Injective, surjective and bijective functions
    - Composition, inverses and restrictions
    - Piecewise definitions and graphs

### Numbers and reasoning

- [ ] Number systems
    - Natural numbers, integers, rationals and real numbers
    - Intervals, absolute value and inequalities
    - Upper bounds, lower bounds and completeness of the real numbers
- [ ] Proof methods
    - Direct proof and proof by cases
    - Contrapositive and contradiction
    - Mathematical induction
    - Counterexamples and the limits of numerical evidence
