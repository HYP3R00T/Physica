## What to study

### Inner products and orthogonality

- [ ] Inner-product spaces
    - Real inner products, norms, angles and distance
    - Cauchy-Schwarz and triangle inequalities
    - Complex inner products and conjugate symmetry after complex numbers
    - Orthogonal complements and orthogonal direct sums
    - Orthogonality relations among the four fundamental subspaces
- [ ] Orthonormal bases
    - Gram-Schmidt orthogonalisation
    - Coordinates in an orthonormal basis
    - Orthogonal matrices and preservation of length
    - QR factorisation and solving triangular systems

### Projections and least squares

- [ ] Orthogonal projections
    - Projection onto a line and onto a subspace
    - Projection matrices and their defining properties
    - Best approximation and orthogonal residuals
- [ ] Least-squares systems
    - Column-space geometry and normal equations
    - Full-column-rank assumptions and uniqueness
    - QR solutions and avoiding unnecessary normal-equation formation
    - Rank-deficient problems as preparation for the pseudoinverse
    - Weighted least squares and fitting data

### Applications

- [ ] Orthogonal expansions
    - Finite function bases and polynomial fitting
    - Discrete Fourier bases after studying complex exponentials
    - Distinguishing a finite basis from completeness in function spaces

## Reading

Lang, Chapter VI; Strang, Chapter 3. Strang develops projections, least squares and QR beyond the geometric starting point in Lang.
