## What to study

### Linear equations and inequalities

- [ ] Equations in one variable
    - Linear form ax + b = 0, degree one and the special case a = 0
    - Unknowns, parameters, solution sets and equality-preserving operations
    - Checking solutions in the original equation
    - Equations with brackets, fractions and decimals
    - Unique solutions, identities and contradictions
    - Rearranging formulas and solving for a parameter
    - Translating word problems into equations
- [ ] Inequalities
    - Order properties and reversal under negative multiplication
    - Compound inequalities, intervals and number-line solutions
    - Absolute-value equations and inequalities by distance and cases

### Coordinates and straight-line graphs

- [ ] Cartesian coordinates

- [ ] Functions in a first example

### Simultaneous linear equations

- [ ] Two-variable systems
    - Intersections and graphical solutions
    - Unique solutions, no solution and infinitely many solutions
    - Interpreting solutions in a model
- [ ] Methods for two-variable systems
    - Graphical solution and its accuracy limits
    - Substitution, elimination and comparison of two expressions for one unknown
    - Equivalent systems and checking a solution in every equation
- [ ] Systems of linear inequalities
    - Boundary lines, open and closed half-planes
    - Intersections of regions and feasible solutions
- [ ] Extension to several equations
    - Three-variable systems and elimination

    - General systems with m equations and n unknowns
    - Distinguishing the number of variables from the degree of an equation
    - From individual equations to the matrix methods studied later
