## What to study

### From vector spaces to modules

- [ ] Modules over rings
    - Module axioms, left and right modules and vector spaces as modules over fields
    - Abelian groups as modules over the integers
    - Submodules, quotient modules, direct sums and module homomorphisms
    - Kernels, images and isomorphism theorems
- [ ] Generators, relations and freeness
    - Free modules, bases and finitely generated modules
    - Torsion elements and why independence behaves differently over a ring
    - Presentations by generators and relations
    - Exact sequences, splitting and direct-sum decompositions

### Structure and canonical forms

- [ ] Finitely generated modules over a principal ideal domain
    - Structure theorem, invariant factors and elementary divisors
    - Smith normal form and integer matrix reduction
    - Classification of finitely generated abelian groups
    - An operator as a module over a polynomial ring
    - Rational canonical form and the connection to Jordan form

### Algebras and products

- [ ] Algebras over a field
    - Compatible scalar multiplication and multiplication in an algebra
    - Polynomial, matrix, quotient and group algebras
    - Algebra homomorphisms and ideals
    - Tensor products of modules, bilinear maps and their universal property
    - Symmetric, exterior and tensor algebras revisited
