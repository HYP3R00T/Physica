## What to study

### Eigenvalue problems

- [ ] Eigenvalues and eigenspaces
    - Invariant directions and the eigenvalue equation
    - Characteristic polynomial and finding eigenspaces
    - Real matrices with complex eigenvalues
    - Algebraic and geometric multiplicity
    - Trace, determinant and eigenvalues
- [ ] Diagonalisation
    - Independence of eigenvectors and an eigenvector basis
    - Similarity transformations and diagonalisation criteria
    - Repeated eigenvalues and defective matrices
    - Cayley-Hamilton theorem
    - Minimal polynomial and its relation to diagonalisation
    - Invariant subspaces, restrictions and invariant direct sums
    - Commuting diagonalizable operators and simultaneous diagonalisation

### Powers and evolution

- [ ] Matrix powers and recurrences
    - Iteration, difference equations and long-term behaviour
    - Markov matrices and stationary states
    - Stability through eigenvalue magnitudes
- [ ] Matrix functions
    - Polynomial functions and the matrix exponential
    - Constant-coefficient differential systems after ordinary differential equations
    - Oscillations and normal modes as a physics application
