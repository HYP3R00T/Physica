## What to study

### Multilinear maps

- [ ] Bilinear and sesquilinear forms
    - Change of basis and matrix representations
    - Symmetry, signature and nondegeneracy
    - Inner products and indefinite metric forms
- [ ] Tensor products
    - Tensor-product bases and dimensions
    - Pure tensors and general tensor sums
    - Multilinear maps and tensor components
    - Scalars, vectors and higher-order tensors

### Tensor operations

- [ ] Covariant and contravariant components
    - Dual bases and transformation laws
    - Tensor order and matrix rank distinctions
    - Summation convention and free and dummy indices
- [ ] Tensor algebra
    - Tensor products and contractions
    - Symmetrisation and antisymmetrisation
    - Raising and lowering indices with a metric
    - Kronecker delta and Levi-Civita symbol

### Exterior and symmetric algebra

- [ ] Alternating tensors
    - Exterior products and alternating forms
    - Determinants, oriented volume and orientation
    - Hodge dual in an oriented inner-product space
- [ ] Symmetric tensors
    - Quadratic forms and principal axes
    - Trace and traceless decomposition
    - Rotational invariants
