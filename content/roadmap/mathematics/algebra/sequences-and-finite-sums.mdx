## What to study

### Sequences and sums

- [ ] Sequences
    - Indexing, explicit terms and recurrence relations
    - Initial conditions and generating successive terms
    - Arithmetic and geometric progressions and their finite sums
- [ ] Finite summation
    - Sigma notation, linearity and changes of index
    - Sums of integers, squares and cubes
    - Telescoping sums and induction for sum identities
    - Distinguishing finite sums from infinite series

### Counting and binomial expansions

- [ ] Counting methods
    - Sum and product rules and avoiding double counting
    - Factorials, permutations and arrangements with repeated objects
    - Combinations and the role of order and repetition
- [ ] Binomial theorem for nonnegative integer powers
    - Binomial coefficients, Pascal's identity and symmetry
    - General term, specified coefficients and finite expansions
    - Combinatorial and inductive arguments
    - Distinguishing finite expansions from infinite binomial series
