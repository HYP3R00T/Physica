## What to study

### From equations to matrices

- [ ] Matrix notation and operations
    - Rows, columns, shape and entries
    - Matrix-vector products as linear combinations of columns
    - Row and column interpretations of a linear system
    - Matrix multiplication, composition and noncommutativity
    - Identity, diagonal, triangular, transpose and permutation matrices
- [ ] Solution structure
    - General m-by-n systems and the matrix equation Ax = b
    - Unique, absent and infinitely many solutions
    - Homogeneous solution spaces and affine nonhomogeneous solution sets
- [ ] Gaussian and Gauss-Jordan elimination
    - Augmented matrices and elementary row operations
    - Pivots, echelon forms and reduced row echelon form
    - Back substitution, free variables and parametric solutions
    - Consistent and inconsistent systems
    - Homogeneous and nonhomogeneous equations
    - A particular solution plus a homogeneous solution

### Invertibility and factorisation

- [ ] Matrix inverses
    - Invertibility, singularity and solving by elimination
    - Elementary matrices and row reduction of an augmented identity
    - Inverses of products and transposes
    - Conditions for uniqueness in a square system
- [ ] LU factorisation
    - Elimination multipliers and triangular factors
    - Row exchanges, pivoting and permutation matrices
    - Solving several systems with the same coefficient matrix
    - Residual checks and why explicit inversion is usually unnecessary

### Applications

- [ ] Systems as models
    - Balance equations and simple networks
    - Rectangular systems and underdetermined or overdetermined data
    - Distinguishing exact solutions from approximation problems

## Reading

Lang, Chapters II and V; Strang, Chapter 1. Work through elimination by hand before using a numerical solver.
