## What to study

### Integer arithmetic used by groups

- [ ] Divisibility and congruences
    - Integer division algorithm, greatest common divisors and Euclidean algorithm
    - Bezout identity, modular arithmetic and multiplicative inverses modulo n
    - Congruence classes and well-defined operations on representatives

### Groups and subgroups

- [ ] Group definitions and examples
    - Operations, closure, associativity, identity and inverses
    - Integers, modular arithmetic, permutations and symmetries
    - Cyclic groups, generators and element orders
    - Subgroup tests and generated subgroups
    - Permutation cycles, parity and dihedral groups
- [ ] Cosets and quotients
    - Cosets, index and Lagrange's theorem
    - Normal subgroups and quotient groups
    - Homomorphisms, kernels, images and isomorphisms
    - First, second and third isomorphism theorems
    - Correspondence theorem and direct products
    - Cayley theorem and permutation representations

### Actions and group structure

- [ ] Group actions
    - Orbits, stabilisers and orbit-stabiliser theorem
    - Conjugation, conjugacy classes and the class equation
    - Rotation, reflection and point groups
    - Burnside counting as an application
- [ ] Further structure
    - Finite abelian groups and their decomposition
    - Sylow theorems and examples
    - Semidirect products, generators, relations and group presentations
    - Commutator subgroups, solvable groups and simple groups
    - Matrix-group examples after studying linear maps
