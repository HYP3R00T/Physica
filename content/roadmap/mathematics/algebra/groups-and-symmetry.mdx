## What to study

### Algebraic structures

- [ ] Groups
    - Group axioms, identity and inverses
    - Cyclic, permutation and matrix groups
    - Subgroups, cosets and Lagrange's theorem
    - Normal subgroups and quotient groups
- [ ] Structure-preserving maps
    - Homomorphisms, kernels and images
    - Isomorphisms and the first isomorphism theorem
    - Rings and fields as related algebraic structures

### Symmetry actions

- [ ] Group actions
    - Orbits, stabilisers and conjugacy classes
    - Rotation, reflection and point groups
    - Direct products and semidirect products
- [ ] Finite-group representations
    - Matrix representations and equivalence
    - Invariant subspaces and irreducibility
    - Unitary representations and Schur's lemma
    - Characters, orthogonality and character tables
    - Tensor products and decomposition into irreducible representations
