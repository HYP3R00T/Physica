## What to study

### Equations and data

- [ ] PDE classification
    - Order, linearity and principal part
    - Elliptic, parabolic and hyperbolic equations
    - Initial, boundary and mixed data
    - Well-posedness and compatibility conditions
- [ ] First-order equations
    - Characteristics and transport equations
    - Characteristic curves and initial data
    - Nonlinear conservation laws and shock formation: introduction

### Canonical linear equations

- [ ] Laplace and Poisson equations
    - Harmonic functions and maximum principles
    - Dirichlet, Neumann and Robin boundary conditions
    - Uniqueness and the Neumann compatibility condition
- [ ] Heat and diffusion equations
    - Initial-boundary problems and smoothing
    - Fundamental heat kernel and diffusion scales
- [ ] Wave equation
    - D'Alembert solution and finite propagation speed
    - Initial displacement and velocity
    - Energy methods and boundary reflections

### Solution methods in several dimensions

- [ ] Separation of variables
    - Cartesian, cylindrical and spherical domains
    - Boundary eigenfunctions and mode expansions
    - Laplace, heat and wave equations in two and three dimensions
- [ ] Integral-transform methods
    - Fourier and Laplace reduction
    - Infinite and semi-infinite domains
- [ ] Inhomogeneous equations
    - Duhamel's principle
    - Eigenfunction expansion of sources
    - Helmholtz and Schrodinger equations as related linear problems
