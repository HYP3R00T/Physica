## What to study

### First-order nonlinear equations

- [ ] Separable equations
    - Implicit solutions and lost equilibrium solutions
    - Finite-time blow-up and intervals of existence
- [ ] Substitution methods
    - Homogeneous first-order equations
    - Bernoulli and reducible Riccati equations
- [ ] Exact differential equations
    - Exactness criteria and potential functions
    - Integrating factors and domain restrictions
- [ ] Existence and uniqueness
    - Local Lipschitz conditions and Picard iteration
    - Continuous dependence on initial data

### Systems of equations

- [ ] Linear systems
    - Fundamental matrices and matrix exponentials
    - Eigenvalues, repeated roots and Jordan blocks
    - Nonhomogeneous systems and variation of constants
- [ ] Autonomous nonlinear systems
    - Equilibria, nullclines and phase portraits
    - Linearisation and local stability
    - Conserved quantities and Lyapunov functions
    - Limit cycles and the Poincare-Bendixson theorem

### Changes in behaviour

- [ ] Bifurcations
    - Saddle-node, transcritical, pitchfork and Hopf bifurcations
    - Parameter dependence and structural stability
- [ ] Nonlinear evolution
    - Poincare maps and discrete dynamical systems
    - Sensitivity to initial conditions and Lyapunov exponents
    - Deterministic chaos and attractors
