## What to study

### Limits and spaces

- [ ] Real-number completeness
    - Supremum and infimum
    - Bolzano-Weierstrass theorem and Cauchy completeness
- [ ] Metric spaces
    - Open and closed sets
    - Convergence and continuity
    - Compactness, connectedness and completeness
    - Uniform continuity
- [ ] Function sequences
    - Uniform convergence and interchange of limiting operations
    - Contraction mappings and fixed-point theorem

### Measure and integration

- [ ] Measure foundations
    - Sigma algebras and measurable sets
    - Lebesgue measure and sets of measure zero
    - Measurable functions and almost-everywhere statements
- [ ] Lebesgue integration
    - Simple functions and integrable functions
    - Relation to Riemann and improper integrals
    - Monotone and dominated convergence theorems
    - Fatou's lemma
    - Tonelli and Fubini theorems
- [ ] Function norms
    - Integrable and square-integrable functions
    - Holder and Minkowski inequalities
    - Convergence in norm and almost everywhere
