## What to study

### Ordinary differential equations

- [ ] Time stepping
    - Euler, Runge-Kutta and multistep methods
    - Adaptive error control
    - Stiffness and implicit methods
    - Symplectic integration and long-time conservation
- [ ] Boundary-value problems
    - Shooting and relaxation methods
    - Collocation and boundary residuals

### Partial differential equations

- [ ] Discretisation
    - Finite differences in space and time
    - Consistency, stability and convergence
    - Von Neumann analysis and Courant conditions
    - Explicit, implicit and Crank-Nicolson schemes
- [ ] Further discretisation methods
    - Finite-volume conservation and fluxes
    - Weak formulations and finite elements
    - Spectral methods and fast Fourier transforms
    - Boundary conditions and grid refinement

### Optimisation and random sampling

- [ ] Numerical optimisation
    - Gradient, Newton and quasi-Newton methods
    - Constraints and convergence diagnostics
- [ ] Monte Carlo methods
    - Pseudorandom sampling and inverse-transform methods
    - Rejection and importance sampling
    - Monte Carlo integration and uncertainty
    - Markov chain Monte Carlo and Metropolis-Hastings
    - Burn-in, autocorrelation and effective sample size
- [ ] Verification
    - Known solutions and manufactured solutions
    - Conservation and dimensional checks
    - Discretisation errors versus sampling errors
