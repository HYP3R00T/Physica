## What to study

### Error and stability

- [ ] Floating-point arithmetic
    - Representation, rounding and machine precision
    - Cancellation, overflow and underflow
    - Absolute and relative errors
- [ ] Conditioning and stability
    - Problem conditioning and algorithmic stability
    - Forward and backward error
    - Convergence order and stopping criteria

### Approximation of functions

- [ ] Root finding
    - Bisection and bracketed methods
    - Newton and secant methods
    - Nonlinear systems and Jacobians
- [ ] Interpolation and extrapolation
    - Lagrange and Newton polynomials
    - Divided differences and interpolation error
    - Runge phenomenon and Chebyshev nodes
    - Piecewise polynomials and cubic splines
    - Richardson extrapolation and its assumptions
- [ ] Numerical differentiation and quadrature
    - Finite differences and step-size selection
    - Trapezoidal and Simpson rules
    - Gaussian and adaptive quadrature
    - Singular and oscillatory integrals

### Numerical linear algebra

- [ ] Linear systems
    - Pivoted LU, Cholesky and QR methods
    - Sparse matrices and iterative solvers
    - Least squares and singular value decomposition
- [ ] Eigenvalue computation
    - Power and inverse iteration
    - QR algorithm: introduction
    - Residuals, conditioning and convergence checks
