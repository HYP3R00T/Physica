## What to study

### Functions of several variables

- [ ] Domains and level sets
    - Scalar-valued and vector-valued functions
    - Limits, continuity and path dependence
- [ ] Partial and total derivatives
    - Directional derivatives and gradients
    - Differentiability and linear approximation
    - Jacobian matrices and the multivariable chain rule
    - Higher derivatives, Hessians and mixed partials
    - Taylor expansion in several variables
- [ ] Implicit and inverse functions
    - Local invertibility and nonsingular Jacobians
    - Implicit differentiation and constraint surfaces

### Optimisation

- [ ] Unconstrained extrema
    - Stationary points and Hessian classification
    - Degenerate cases and boundary checks
- [ ] Constrained extrema
    - Lagrange multipliers
    - Several constraints and regularity conditions
    - Inequality constraints: introduction

### Multiple integrals

- [ ] Double and triple integrals
    - Regions, iterated integrals and changing integration order
    - Fubini's theorem and integrability conditions
    - Improper multiple integrals
- [ ] Changes of variables
    - Jacobian determinants and integration measures
    - Polar, cylindrical and spherical coordinates
    - Mass, moments and centres of mass
- [ ] Parameter-dependent integrals
    - Differentiation under the integral sign
    - Moving limits and sufficient regularity conditions
