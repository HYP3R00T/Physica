## What to study

### Sequences and infinite sums

- [ ] Sequence convergence
    - Limits, boundedness and monotonicity
    - Subsequences and Cauchy sequences
    - Limit superior and limit inferior
- [ ] Infinite series
    - Partial sums and convergence
    - Geometric and telescoping series
    - Comparison, integral, ratio and root tests
    - Alternating series and remainder bounds
    - Absolute and conditional convergence
    - Rearrangements and products of series

### Function expansions

- [ ] Power series
    - Radius and interval of convergence
    - Endpoint tests
    - Termwise differentiation and integration
- [ ] Taylor and Maclaurin expansions
    - Taylor's theorem and remainder estimates
    - Standard elementary-function expansions
    - Convergence to the function and smooth nonanalytic functions
    - Generalised binomial series
- [ ] Asymptotic approximations
    - Order notation and dominant balance
    - Convergent series and asymptotic series
    - Truncation error and useful approximation ranges

### Sequences of functions

- [ ] Pointwise and uniform convergence
    - Uniform convergence tests
    - Conditions for interchanging limits, derivatives and integrals
    - Uniform convergence of power series on compact subintervals
