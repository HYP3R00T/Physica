## What to study

### Fields and differential operators

- [ ] Scalar and vector fields
    - Field lines, level surfaces and potentials
    - Gradient, divergence, curl and Laplacian
    - Product rules and vector identities
- [ ] Curvilinear coordinates
    - Orthogonal coordinates and scale factors
    - Basis-vector variation
    - Differential operators in cylindrical and spherical coordinates

### Integrals over curves and surfaces

- [ ] Line integrals
    - Parametrisation and orientation
    - Scalar arc-length integrals and vector circulation
    - Conservative fields and path independence
    - Simply connected domains and singularities
- [ ] Surface integrals
    - Parametrised surfaces and area elements
    - Normal orientation and flux
    - Closed surfaces and enclosed volumes

### Integral theorems

- [ ] Green's theorem in the plane
- [ ] Divergence theorem
- [ ] Stokes' theorem
    - Boundary orientation and regularity assumptions
    - Multiply connected regions and excluded singularities
- [ ] Green's identities
    - Integration by parts in several dimensions
    - Uniqueness and boundary terms
- [ ] Helmholtz decomposition
    - Longitudinal and transverse fields
    - Boundary conditions and decay assumptions
