## What to study

### Plane geometry

- [ ] Cartesian coordinates
    - Distances, midpoints and section formulas
    - Slopes, intercepts and equations of lines
    - Angles, intersections and distances from lines
- [ ] Circles and conics
    - Circle equations, tangents and normals
    - Focus-directrix definitions and eccentricity
    - Parabolas, ellipses and hyperbolas
    - Standard forms, asymptotes and intersections

### Alternative coordinates

- [ ] Parametric curves
    - Parametric lines, circles and conics
    - Eliminating parameters and tracing curves
- [ ] Polar coordinates
    - Cartesian conversion and nonunique representations
    - Polar equations of conics
    - Symmetry and elementary polar curves
- [ ] Spatial coordinates
    - Cartesian, cylindrical and spherical coordinates
    - Spheres and basic quadric surfaces
    - Coordinate translation and rotation
