## What to study

### Multivariate probability and data

- [ ] Joint distributions
    - Marginal and conditional distributions
    - Independence, covariance and correlation
    - Multivariate normal distribution
    - Variable transformations and Jacobians
- [ ] Sampling and descriptive statistics
    - Sample moments, histograms and empirical distributions
    - Sampling bias and independent observations
    - Standard error and sample size

### Estimation and uncertainty

- [ ] Parameter estimation
    - Likelihood and maximum likelihood
    - Bias, consistency and efficiency
    - Fisher information and Cramer-Rao bound
    - Bayesian priors, posteriors and credible intervals
- [ ] Uncertainty analysis
    - Random and systematic effects
    - Propagation through functions with covariance
    - Confidence intervals and coverage
    - Bootstrap resampling

### Fitting and testing

- [ ] Regression and curve fitting
    - Linear least squares and weighted least squares
    - Correlated uncertainties and generalised least squares
    - Nonlinear fitting and parameter covariance
    - Residual analysis and model adequacy
- [ ] Hypothesis tests
    - Null and alternative hypotheses
    - Significance, power and multiple testing
    - Chi-square goodness-of-fit and degrees of freedom
    - Likelihood-ratio tests
    - Prediction, interpolation and extrapolation uncertainty
