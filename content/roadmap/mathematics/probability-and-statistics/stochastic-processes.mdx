## What to study

### Random evolution

    - Transition matrices and Chapman-Kolmogorov equations
    - Stationary distributions and detailed balance
    - Recurrence, absorption and first-passage times
- [ ] Continuous-time jump processes
    - Poisson processes and waiting times

    - Fokker-Planck and backward equations

    - Ornstein-Uhlenbeck process
    - First-passage and escape problems
