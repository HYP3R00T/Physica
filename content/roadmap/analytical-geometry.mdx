## What to study

### Plane geometry

- [ ] Cartesian coordinates
  - Coordinate axes
  - Distance formula
  - Midpoint and section formulas
  - Translation of origin
- [ ] Straight lines
  - Slope and intercept forms
  - Point-slope and general forms
  - Parallel and perpendicular lines
  - Angles between lines
  - Point-to-line distance
  - Intersections and concurrency
  - Angle bisectors
- [ ] Triangle centres
  - Centroid
  - Orthocentre
  - Incentre
  - Circumcentre
- [ ] Circles
  - Standard and general equations
  - Centre and radius
  - Tangents, normals and chords
  - Line–circle and circle–circle intersections
- [ ] Conic sections
  - Parabola, ellipse and hyperbola
  - Standard equations
  - Foci and directrices
  - Eccentricity
  - Tangents and normals
- [ ] Parametric curves
  - Parameters and orientation
  - Parametric lines, circles and conics
  - Elimination of parameters
- [ ] Polar coordinates
  - Radius and polar angle
  - Cartesian–polar conversion
  - Polar equations of conics
- [ ] Loci
  - Geometric constraints
  - Equations of loci

### Spatial geometry

- [ ] Three-dimensional Cartesian coordinates
  - Coordinate planes
  - Distance between points
  - Direction cosines and direction ratios
- [ ] Lines in space
  - Parametric and symmetric equations
  - Intersecting, parallel and skew lines
  - Coplanarity
  - Angles and shortest distances
- [ ] Planes
  - Plane equations
  - Normal direction
  - Point-to-plane distance
  - Angles between planes
  - Angles between lines and planes
