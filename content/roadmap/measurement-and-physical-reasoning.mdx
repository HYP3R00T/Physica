## What to study

- [ ] Units, dimensions, dimensional homogeneity, scaling and estimation.
- [ ] Observables, operational definitions, system boundaries, idealizations and model assumptions.
- [ ] Measurement resolution, random and systematic errors, significant figures and elementary uncertainty propagation.
- [ ] Tables, graphs, fitting a straight line, residuals and comparison of a prediction with data.

## By the end

Estimate quantities, check dimensions and compare measurements with a model using meaningful uncertainty.
