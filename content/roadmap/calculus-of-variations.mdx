## What to study

- [ ] Functionals and variations
  - Admissible functions
  - Fixed and free endpoints
  - First variation
- [ ] Euler–Lagrange equations
  - One and several dependent functions
  - Functionals with higher derivatives
  - Boundary terms and natural boundary conditions
- [ ] Constraints
  - Integral constraints
  - Lagrange multipliers
- [ ] Stationary points
  - Second variation
  - Minima, maxima and saddle points
- [ ] Variational principles
  - Stationary action
  - Conserved quantities and cyclic variables
  - Noether's theorem
- [ ] Field variations
  - Several independent variables
  - Euler–Lagrange equations for fields
