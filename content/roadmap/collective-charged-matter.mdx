## What to study

- [ ] Quasineutrality, Debye shielding, plasma frequency and collective behaviour.
- [ ] Charged-particle gyromotion, drifts, magnetic confinement ideas and adiabatic invariants.
- [ ] Fluid and kinetic descriptions; Vlasov equation and elementary magnetohydrodynamics.
- [ ] Plasma waves, Alfvén waves, basic instabilities and introductory Landau damping.
- [ ] Space, astrophysical and laboratory plasmas as contrasting applications.

## By the end

Estimate plasma scales and derive elementary wave and drift results across fluid and kinetic descriptions.
