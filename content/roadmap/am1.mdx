## What to study

- [ ] Hydrogen fine/hyperfine structure, Zeeman/Stark shifts, line strengths and selection rules.
- [ ] Many-electron atoms, central-field and Hartree–Fock ideas, exchange, LS/jj coupling and the periodic structure of atoms.
- [ ] Born–Oppenheimer approximation, bonding and molecular rotational/vibrational/electronic spectra.
- [ ] Franck–Condon factors, Raman/infrared spectroscopy, line broadening and lifetimes.

## By the end

Interpret simple atomic and molecular spectra and estimate splittings, transition strengths and lifetimes.
