## What to study

- [ ] Choose a substantial established result involving at least two subjects, such as phonon heat capacity, radiative transitions, plasma waves or stellar degeneracy pressure.
- [ ] Reconstruct its assumptions and central derivation without treating the source as an authority to copy.
- [ ] Reproduce a numerical result or analyse a relevant public experimental dataset; quantify errors and limitations.
- [ ] Write a short technical explanation with sources, dimensions, limiting cases and unresolved questions.
- [ ] Identify the next specialist topics needed to go further and distinguish them from gaps in the shared core.

## By the end

Reproduce a bounded established result spanning two subjects. Also complete whichever branch segments the chosen result actually uses; no need to finish all bridges first.
