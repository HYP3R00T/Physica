## What to study

- [ ] Sinusoidal waves, phase, wavelength, frequency, propagation speed, energy and intensity.
- [ ] Superposition, beats, standing waves, reflection, transmission and boundary conditions.
- [ ] Sound, pressure waves, acoustic intensity, Doppler shifts and resonance in strings and air columns.
- [ ] Introduce partial-derivative notation for a displacement depending on position and time; verify travelling-wave solutions of the one-dimensional wave equation. General PDE solution methods come later.

## By the end

Calculate superpositions and standing-wave modes and distinguish wave speed from material velocity.
