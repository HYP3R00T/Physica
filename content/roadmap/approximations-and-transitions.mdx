## What to study

- [ ] Nondegenerate and degenerate stationary perturbation theory, including second-order corrections where appropriate.
- [ ] Variational methods and WKB, turning points and tunnelling estimates.
- [ ] Time-dependent perturbation theory, transition amplitudes and Fermi's golden rule.
- [ ] Driven two-level systems, Rabi oscillations and the adiabatic approximation.
- [ ] Selection rules, dipole coupling and elementary emission/absorption calculations.
- [ ] Introductory scattering amplitudes, Born approximation and differential cross sections.

## By the end

Select and justify perturbative, variational or semiclassical methods and calculate a transition or scattering estimate.
