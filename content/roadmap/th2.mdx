## What to study

- [ ] State variables, equations of state, extensive/intensive quantities and exact versus inexact differentials.
- [ ] Entropy, reversible and irreversible processes, thermodynamic identities and the third law.
- [ ] Legendre transforms; Helmholtz/Gibbs potentials, enthalpy, chemical potential and Maxwell relations.
- [ ] Stability, response coefficients, phase coexistence and the Clausius–Clapeyron relation.
- [ ] Mixtures, particle exchange, chemical equilibrium and thermodynamic constraints on reactions.

## By the end

Choose thermodynamic potentials, derive response identities and analyse phase and chemical equilibrium.
