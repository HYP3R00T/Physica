- [ ] Brownian motion, diffusion, fluctuations and low-Reynolds-number motion.

- [ ] Membranes, osmotic effects, molecular interactions and self-assembly.
- [ ] Reaction–diffusion, transport, molecular motors and energy use away from equilibrium.
