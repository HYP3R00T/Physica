## What to study

- [ ] Nuclear size, binding energy, isotopes, stability and semi-empirical mass formula.
- [ ] Nuclear forces, the deuteron, scattering and shell/collective models.
- [ ] Alpha, beta and gamma decay, selection rules, lifetimes and decay chains.
- [ ] Reaction kinematics, cross sections, resonances, fission and fusion.
- [ ] Interaction of radiation with matter and principles of nuclear detection.

## By the end

Calculate decay and reaction observables and explain what nuclear models and detectors establish.
