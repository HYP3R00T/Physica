## What to study

- [ ] Linear equations
  - Homogeneous and inhomogeneous equations
  - Superposition
  - Fundamental solutions
  - Wronskian
- [ ] Second-order constant-coefficient equations
  - Characteristic equation
  - Distinct, repeated and complex roots
- [ ] Particular solutions
  - Undetermined coefficients
  - Variation of parameters
  - Resonant forcing
- [ ] Oscillatory solutions
  - Underdamped, critically damped and overdamped solutions
  - Transient and steady-state solutions
  - Sinusoidal forcing
  - Complex amplitude and phase
- [ ] Coupled linear systems
  - Matrix representation
  - Eigenvalue methods
  - Normal coordinates
  - Matrix exponentials
  - Initial-value problems
- [ ] Higher-order equations
  - Reduction to first-order systems
- [ ] Series solutions
  - Ordinary and regular singular points
  - Power-series solutions
  - Frobenius method
