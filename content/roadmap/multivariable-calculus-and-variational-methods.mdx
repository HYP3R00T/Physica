## What to study

- [ ] Multivariable derivatives, total differentials, Taylor expansions and constrained extrema.
- [ ] Variational calculus, functionals, stationary action and Euler–Lagrange equations.
- [ ] Legendre transformations as changes of independent variables; tensor components and coordinate transformations at introductory level.

## By the end

Use partial derivatives and constrained extrema; derive the Euler–Lagrange equation without assuming every stationary point is a minimum.
