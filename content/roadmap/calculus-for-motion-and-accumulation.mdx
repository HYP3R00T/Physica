## What to study

- [ ] Limits and continuity through graphs and elementary calculations; the distinction between average and instantaneous change.
- [ ] Derivatives from the difference quotient; tangent slope; notation d/dx, dy/dx and d²y/dx²; units of a derivative.
- [ ] Derivatives of powers, trigonometric, exponential and logarithmic functions; sum, product, quotient and chain rules; implicit differentiation.
- [ ] Definite integration as signed accumulation; antiderivatives, integration constants, the fundamental theorem of calculus and initial conditions.
- [ ] Substitution, integration by parts and simple partial fractions; improper integrals in elementary examples.
- [ ] Extrema, local linear approximation and elementary Taylor expansions with a remainder/error interpretation; geometric series and basic convergence.
- [ ] First differential equations: dy/dt = f(t), separable growth/decay and d²x/dt² = constant. Interpret a solution and its initial conditions.

## By the end

Differentiate and integrate common functions, interpret rates and accumulated changes, and recover a function from a rate and an initial value. Full real analysis is outside this segment.
